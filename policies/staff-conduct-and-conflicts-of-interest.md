# Staff Conduct and Conflicts of Interest

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## Conflicts of interest

Staff must declare to their manager, in writing, any personal or family link to a customer, borrower, vendor or
job applicant they deal with, and take no part in decisions about them. This includes loans, waivers, rate
concessions and hiring.

## Own accounts

Staff never process transactions on their own accounts or those of relatives, and never look up a relative's,
colleague's or public figure's data out of curiosity. Every look-up is logged and audited.

## Gifts

Gifts from customers or vendors above Rs 2,000 in value are declined or declared to HR.

## Speaking up

Staff who see a breach of policy report it to their manager, HR or Compliance. Reports made in good faith are
protected from retaliation.
