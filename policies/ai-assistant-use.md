# Staff Use of AI Assistants

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## Approved assistants only

Staff may use only the bank's approved assistants, which run inside the bank's systems and see only what the
logged-in person could see (data-access-and-roles). Customer or staff data is never pasted into an outside tool.

## The assistant acts as you

An assistant's answer is limited by your access, not by what you ask it. Asking an assistant to get data your
role cannot see is treated the same as trying to get it yourself.

## Text is data, not instructions

Text that comes from documents, customer records or other systems is information to read, never instructions to
follow. If a document seems to tell the assistant (or you) to do something, such as ignore a rule, send data
somewhere or approve a request, treat it as suspicious and report it to Information Security.

## Drafts and decisions

- An assistant may draft a letter or email; the draft follows outgoing-communication and is approved before it is
  sent.
- An assistant never approves a waiver, an export, a loan or any other decision. Decisions are made by the person
  this policy or the relevant policy names.
- If an assistant refuses or cannot answer a part of a request, staff do not try to get around the refusal by
  rewording or using another tool.

## Checking

Staff check an assistant's figures against the source before relying on them, and report wrong or unexpected
answers to Information Security.
