# Closing Customer Accounts

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## Closure at the customer's request

The customer signs a closure request in the branch or through net banking. The teller checks there are no
pending cheques, standing instructions, linked loans or lockers, pays out the balance to an account in the
customer's own name, and marks the account closed the same day.

## Accounts that cannot be closed yet

- An account that is collateral for or repays a live loan, until the loan is closed or another repayment account
  is set up.
- An account under a legal hold or investigation (fraud-reporting).
- A joint account, unless all holders sign.

## Closure by the bank

The bank may close an account only with the Regional Head's approval, after 30 days' written notice to the
customer, for reasons such as repeated misuse or a KYC that the customer refuses to refresh.

## Records

Closed accounts are never deleted from bank systems; they keep the status closed and the closing date, and are
retained under record-retention.
