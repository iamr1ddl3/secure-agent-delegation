# Salary and Compensation Confidentiality

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## What is covered

Annual CTC (cost to company), salary history, bonuses, increments, and any figure from which an individual's pay
could be worked out.

## Individual salary data

An individual's salary may be seen only by:

- the employee themselves;
- their **Branch Manager**, for staff of their own branch only;
- **Human Resources**;
- **Internal Audit**, for audit work.

It is never discussed with or passed to other staff, including other managers, and never sent outside the bank
except to the employee or where the law requires it, through HR.

## Aggregate salary data

Averages or totals by department, branch or grade may be shared with the bank's leadership and with the Board's
remuneration committee, provided each group contains at least **5** employees. Smaller groups are combined or
left out so that no one's pay can be inferred.

## How it is sent

Salary data is sent only as a password-protected file through bank email or the HR system. Never by personal
email, messaging apps, printed copies left on desks, or in a meeting slide shown to a mixed audience.

## Breaches

Sharing salary data outside these rules is a disciplinary matter and must be reported to HR on the day it is
noticed.
