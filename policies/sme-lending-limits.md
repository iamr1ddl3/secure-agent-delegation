# SME Lending Limits and Approvals

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## Sanctioning authority

| Total exposure to one SME borrower (all facilities) | Sanctioned by |
| --- | --- |
| Up to Rs 50,00,000 | Branch Manager, with the credit officer's appraisal |
| Rs 50,00,000 to Rs 2,00,00,000 | Regional Head |
| Above Rs 2,00,00,000 | Head Office Credit Committee |

## Requirements

Two years of financial statements, the borrower's bank statements for 12 months, and a site visit for any new
borrower. Collateral cover of at least 100% for loans above Rs 25,00,000, unless covered by an approved guarantee
scheme.

## Monitoring

SME loans are reviewed annually. A missed instalment follows overdue-loan-handling; an SME loan more than 90 days
past due is an NPA like any other.

## Conflicts

A staff member who has any personal or family link to the borrower must declare it and take no part in the
decision (staff-conduct-and-conflicts-of-interest).
