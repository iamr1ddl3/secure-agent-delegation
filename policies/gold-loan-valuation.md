# Gold Loans: Valuation and Loan-to-Value

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## Valuation

Gold ornaments are valued at the branch by an approved valuer, in front of the customer, using the bank's reference
gold price for that day. Stones and non-gold parts are excluded.

## Loan-to-value

The loan may not exceed **75%** of the assessed value of the gold. If the gold price falls so that the loan exceeds
the limit, the customer is asked to repay part of the loan or add gold within 30 days.

## Custody

Pledged gold is sealed in the customer's presence and kept in the branch vault under dual control
(cash-handling-limits). It is returned only to the borrower on full repayment, against the pledge receipt.

## Overdue gold loans

A gold loan follows overdue-loan-handling; an auction of pledged gold is a recovery step under
npa-classification-and-recovery and needs at least 14 days' written notice to the borrower.
