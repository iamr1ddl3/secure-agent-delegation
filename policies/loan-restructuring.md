# Restructuring Loans for Borrowers in Hardship

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## When it is offered

A restructuring may be offered to a borrower with a genuine, documented hardship (job loss, illness, business
disruption) who can show they will be able to pay under new terms. It is considered from day 61 past due
(overdue-loan-handling) or at the borrower's request at any time.

## Options

Longer tenure, a moratorium on principal of up to 6 months (interest continues), or a lower instalment for up to
12 months followed by a catch-up. The interest rate is not cut below the bank's cost of funds.

## Who approves

| Loan | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Overdue (not NPA), outstanding up to Rs 25,00,000 | Branch Manager |
| Overdue, outstanding above Rs 25,00,000 | Regional Head |
| Any NPA loan | Regional Recovery Unit, then the Regional Head (npa-classification-and-recovery) |

## Conditions

A loan may be restructured at most once in any 3 years. The borrower signs revised terms; the file records the
hardship evidence and the approver. Restructuring does not by itself change an NPA's classification.
