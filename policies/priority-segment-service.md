# Priority Segment Service Standards

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## Who is priority

Customers with total relationship value (deposits plus investments) of Rs 25,00,000 or more, or a monthly salary
credit of Rs 3,00,000 or more. The segment is shown on the customer record as priority.

## Service standards

A named relationship contact at the branch, service requests acknowledged within 4 business hours, and the
priority counter used where the branch has one.

## Fees

Priority customers receive the account-charge waiver limits in fee-waiver-authority: a teller may waive account
charges up to Rs 1,000 per customer per quarter for a priority customer (Rs 500 for others). Larger waivers, and
**any loan charge**, go to the Branch Manager. Priority status does not by itself entitle a customer to a waiver.

## What priority does not change

Priority customers follow the same KYC, transaction review and data-sharing rules as everyone else. Staff never
share one priority customer's details with another, including in referrals.
