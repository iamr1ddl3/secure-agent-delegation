# Personal Data Masking and Bulk Exports

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## Raw identifiers

National ID numbers, phone numbers and full account numbers are **raw identifiers**. Where a role or a report does
not need the full value, the masked form is used (for example XXXXXX2457).

## Bulk exports need a second person

A list of **more than 20 rows** that includes raw identifiers is a bulk export. It is not released straight to the
person who asked. Instead:

1. The request is held for review.
2. A different Branch Manager or an Internal Auditor approves or rejects it. **The approver never sees the data**;
   they decide on the request, the reason and the query.
3. Once approved, only the person who asked collects the result, once.

Requesters may not approve their own exports.

## Storage

Exported files are kept on bank systems only and deleted when the purpose is met, under record-retention.
