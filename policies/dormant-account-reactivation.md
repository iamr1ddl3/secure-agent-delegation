# Dormant Accounts and Reactivation

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## When an account becomes dormant

A savings or current account becomes **dormant** after 24 months with no customer-initiated transaction. Interest
credits and bank charges do not count as activity. The account status changes to dormant automatically.

## What changes

Debits are blocked; credits are still accepted. No charges for minimum balance are applied to a dormant account.
The customer is told by letter within 30 days of the account turning dormant.

## Reactivation

1. The customer visits the branch or uses video KYC; if their KYC has expired, kyc-refresh applies first.
2. The teller verifies identity, takes a signed reactivation request, and records the reason the account was
   unused.
3. Accounts with a balance above Rs 5,00,000 also need the Branch Manager's approval, to guard against
   impersonation.

## Fraud watch

The first three debits after reactivation are reviewed by the branch the same day. A request to change the phone
number or address at the same visit as reactivation needs the Branch Manager's approval.

## Unclaimed deposits

Accounts dormant for 10 years are transferred to the unclaimed deposits process run by Head Office; the customer
or their heirs can still claim the money at any time.
