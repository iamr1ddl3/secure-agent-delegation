# Fee and Charge Waiver Authority

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## Purpose

Sets out who may waive or reverse a fee or charge, up to what amount, and how every waiver is recorded. A waiver
granted outside this table is a breach of policy even if the customer was right to complain.

## Account charges

Account charges are fees on savings, current and fixed deposit accounts: minimum-balance charges, cheque book
charges, SMS alert fees, and cash-handling fees.

| Role | Limit per customer per calendar quarter |
| --- | --- |
| Teller | Up to Rs 500; up to Rs 1,000 for priority-segment customers |
| Branch Manager | Up to Rs 10,000 |
| Regional Head | Above Rs 10,000 |

A teller who would go over their limit must refer the request to the Branch Manager; the teller does not split one
request into several smaller waivers.

## Loan charges

Loan charges are late-payment fees, bounce charges on loan instalments, and prepayment charges.

- **Tellers may not waive any loan charge**, for any customer segment. They pass the request to the Branch Manager.
- The **Branch Manager** may waive late-payment and bounce charges up to Rs 5,000 per loan per financial year.
- Above Rs 5,000 per loan per financial year, or for any loan classified as NPA, only the **Regional Head** may
  waive, on a written note from the Branch Manager.
- Prepayment charges are set by the loan agreement and may only be waived by the Regional Head.

## Recording a waiver

Every waiver, however small, is entered in the branch waiver register on the same business day with: the customer
ID, the account or loan ID, the amount, a reason code (service failure, hardship, goodwill, bank error), and the
name of the person who approved it. A waiver for a bank error also needs the incident reference.

## Reasons that are never accepted

A waiver may not be granted to meet a sales target, as a favour to a relative or colleague (see
staff-conduct-and-conflicts-of-interest), or because a customer threatened to complain.
