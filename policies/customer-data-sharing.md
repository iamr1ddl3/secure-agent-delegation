# Sharing Customer and Business Data

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## Three kinds of data

| Kind | Example | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer-level data | A customer's name, phone, account number, balance, loan | Need to know only, inside the bank |
| Aggregate figures | Loan counts by status, branch totals, average balances | Inside the bank; outside only as below |
| Public figures | Figures already in the bank's published reports | May be shared |

## Inside the bank

Customer-level data is shared only with colleagues who need it for their job and whose role already allows it.
Forwarding data to a colleague whose role could not see it is a breach, even inside the bank.

## Outside the bank

- **Customer-level data is never shared outside the bank**, except to the customer themselves, or where the law
  requires it, through Compliance.
- **Aggregate figures** may be shared outside the bank (for example with a partner, vendor or in a conference
  slide) only if: every group has at least **5** customers, accounts or loans (smaller groups are suppressed), and
  the **Head of Compliance** and **Corporate Communications** have both approved it in writing first.
- Interns, trainees and contractors may prepare aggregate material but may not approve or send it outside.

## How data is sent

Only through bank systems and official email. Never through personal email, messaging apps, or by uploading to an
outside tool that has not been approved (see ai-assistant-use).
