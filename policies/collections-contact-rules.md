# Contacting Customers About Overdue Payments

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## Allowed channels

Phone, SMS, email from the branch's official address, letter, and a branch visit by appointment. Messaging apps
on a personal phone, social media and personal email accounts are never used.

## Allowed hours

Between **8:00 and 19:00** in the customer's local time, Monday to Saturday. No contact on Sundays or on national
public holidays, except to reply to a customer who asked to be called.

## How often

At most **three calls in any seven days** about the same overdue amount, and at most one visit a week. A customer
who asks for contact in writing only must be contacted in writing only from then on.

## Tone and content

- Polite and factual: the amount due, the due date, the ways to pay, and who to contact for help.
- No threats, no shaming, no language about legal action unless a formal notice has been approved.
- Never discuss the debt with anyone except the borrower, a guarantor, or a person the borrower named in writing.
  Family members, neighbours and employers are not contacted.

## Written reminders and emails

Every written reminder or email to a customer about an overdue payment must use the approved template and be
**approved by the Branch Manager before it is sent** (see outgoing-communication). A draft prepared with an AI
assistant follows the same rule (see ai-assistant-use).

## Records

Every contact attempt and its outcome is noted on the loan file on the same day.
