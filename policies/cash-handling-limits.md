# Cash Handling Limits at Branches

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## Teller limits

| Limit | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Cash a teller may hold at the counter | Rs 5,00,000 |
| Single cash payment a teller may make without a second signature | Rs 50,000 |
| Single cash payment needing the Branch Manager's signature | Above Rs 50,000 |

Cash above the counter limit is moved to the strong room under dual control during the day.

## End of day

Every teller balances their cash at close with a second staff member. A shortage or excess above Rs 1,000 is
reported to the Branch Manager the same day; any shortage above Rs 10,000 is reported to the Regional Head.

## Branch vault

The vault is opened only by two key holders together. The branch's insured cash limit is set by Head Office and
must not be exceeded overnight.
