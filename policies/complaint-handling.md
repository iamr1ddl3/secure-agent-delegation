# Handling Customer Complaints

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## Logging

Every complaint, whether in person, by phone, email or letter, is logged in the complaints system the same day with
a reference number given to the customer. Staff never discourage a customer from complaining.

## Time limits

| Stage | Time limit |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledge | 1 business day |
| Resolve at branch | 7 days |
| Escalate to the Regional Head if unresolved | Day 15 (escalation-to-regional-head) |
| Final response from the bank | 30 days |

## Resolution

A fair resolution may include an apology, a correction, a fee reversal within fee-waiver-authority, or
compensation for a proven bank error (Regional Head approval above Rs 10,000). The customer is told in writing what
was decided and how to take the matter further if they are not satisfied.

## Learning

Branch Managers review complaint themes monthly; repeated causes are reported to Customer Service at Head Office.
