# Record Retention

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## Periods

| Record | Kept for |
| --- | --- |
| Account opening and KYC records | 10 years after the relationship ends |
| Transaction records | 10 years after the transaction |
| Loan files, including restructuring and recovery | 10 years after the loan is closed or written off |
| Complaint files | 7 years after closure |
| Outgoing customer communications | 8 years |
| Bulk export files | Until the stated purpose is met, at most 90 days |

## Rules

Records are kept on bank systems, never on personal devices. Nothing is destroyed while a legal hold, investigation
or audit is open. Destruction at the end of a period is logged.
