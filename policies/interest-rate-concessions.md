# Interest Rate Concessions on Loans and Deposits

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## Card rates

Loan and deposit rates are set by Treasury and published as card rates. Staff quote card rates unless a concession
below is approved.

## Concession limits

| Concession | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Loan rate up to 0.25 percentage points below card | Branch Manager |
| Loan rate 0.25 to 0.75 points below card | Regional Head |
| More than 0.75 points below card | Treasury |
| Fixed deposit rate up to 0.10 points above card, deposits of Rs 25,00,000 or more | Branch Manager |
| Anything higher on deposits | Treasury |

## Conditions

Concessions are for new business or retention with a documented reason; they are not given to staff, their
relatives, or to clear a sales target (staff-conduct-and-conflicts-of-interest). Every concession is recorded on the
loan or deposit file with the approver's name.

## Changing rates in bulk

No branch may change the rates of existing loans or deposits in bulk. Bulk changes are made only by Treasury in the
core banking system.
