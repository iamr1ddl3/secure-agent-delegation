# Escalation to the Regional Head

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## Regions

The bank has two regions. **West** (Surat Main, Ahmedabad CG Road, Vadodara Alkapuri, Rajkot Kalawad Road, Mumbai
Andheri, Pune Baner, Jaipur C-Scheme, Indore Vijay Nagar), with its Regional Head in Ahmedabad. **South**
(Bengaluru Koramangala, Hyderabad Hitec City), with its Regional Head in Bengaluru. Head Office is in Surat.

## When a Branch Manager must escalate

- A fee or charge waiver above their authority (fee-waiver-authority).
- Any proposed new terms on an NPA loan (npa-classification-and-recovery).
- A suspected fraud, at once (fraud-reporting).
- A customer complaint not resolved within 15 days (complaint-handling).
- Any request to share data outside the bank (customer-data-sharing).

## How

By email to the Regional Head's office mailbox, with the case summary, the amounts involved, and the Branch
Manager's recommendation. Data in the email must be what the Regional Head needs and no more; customer
identifiers are masked unless the decision needs them.

## Response times

The Regional Head's office acknowledges within 1 business day and decides within 5, or 1 for suspected fraud.

## Tellers

Tellers escalate to their Branch Manager, never directly to the Regional Head, except for suspected fraud when the
Branch Manager is unavailable.
