# Reporting Suspected Fraud

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## Report at once

Any member of staff who suspects fraud (forged documents, impersonation, an account takeover, or a colleague's
misconduct) reports it **the same day** to their Branch Manager and to Compliance (Financial Crime). If the Branch
Manager may be involved, report to Compliance directly.

## What to do and not do

- Do: preserve the documents and the transaction details; note what was seen and when.
- Do not: confront the suspect, tell the customer that a report has been made, or investigate alone.

## Holds

Compliance may place a hold on an account or transaction while it investigates. A hold is lifted only by
Compliance.

## Escalation

Suspected fraud above Rs 1,00,000, or any involving a member of staff, is reported to the Regional Head within 1
business day (escalation-to-regional-head).
