# Review of High-Value and Unusual Transactions

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## Thresholds

| Transaction | Review |
| --- | --- |
| Cash deposit or withdrawal of Rs 2,00,000 or more in one day | Teller records the source or purpose; Branch Manager signs off the same day |
| Any single transfer of Rs 10,00,000 or more from a retail account | Branch Manager confirms with the customer by phone before release |
| Several cash deposits just under Rs 2,00,000 within a week | Flag to Compliance as possible structuring |
| Large credit into a reactivated dormant account | Same-day branch review (dormant-account-reactivation) |

## Unusual patterns

Staff flag to Compliance any pattern that does not fit the customer's profile: sudden high turnover in a small
retail account, many incoming transfers from unrelated people, or a customer reluctant to explain a transaction.
Flagging is confidential: the customer is never told.

## Who reviews

Compliance (Financial Crime) reviews every flag within 3 business days. Internal Audit samples the branch sign-offs
each quarter.
