# Handling Overdue Loans (1 to 90 Days Past Due)

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## Definitions

A loan is **overdue** when an instalment is 1 to 90 days past due. On day 91 it becomes a non-performing asset
(NPA) and is handled under npa-classification-and-recovery instead. Overdue and NPA loans are counted separately in
every report.

## Owner

The Branch Manager of the loan's home branch owns every overdue loan at that branch and must review the branch's
overdue list every Monday.

## Follow-up steps by days past due

| Days past due | Step | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 7 | Automated SMS and email reminder of the missed instalment | System |
| 8 to 30 | Phone call to the customer; outcome noted on the loan file | Branch staff named by the Branch Manager |
| 31 to 60 | Written reminder letter; branch visit if the customer cannot be reached by phone | Branch Manager or delegate |
| 61 to 90 | Branch Manager review: offer a restructuring assessment (loan-restructuring) if the customer has a genuine hardship; otherwise a final notice that the loan will be classified NPA on day 91 | Branch Manager |

Every contact must follow collections-contact-rules.

## What must not happen

No overdue loan may be "refreshed" by a new loan to the same customer to clear the arrears. No late-payment fee
may be waived except under fee-waiver-authority.

## Reporting

The Branch Manager reports the count and total outstanding of overdue loans to the Regional Head in the monthly
branch review, split into the four bands above.
