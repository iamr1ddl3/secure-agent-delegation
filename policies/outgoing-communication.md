# Outgoing Communication to Customers and Third Parties

> Synthetic document written for the Secure Multi-Agent Delegation project. The bank, its rules and its numbers are invented. Not a real bank's policy and not legal or regulatory advice.

## Scope

Any email, letter, SMS campaign or document sent from the bank to a customer, partner, vendor or regulator.
Internal email between staff is not covered, except where it forwards customer data (customer-data-sharing).

## Approved templates

Reminders, notices and routine letters use the approved templates in the branch template library. A template may
be personalised (name, amounts, dates) but its legal wording may not be changed.

## Who approves before sending

| Communication | Approver before it is sent |
| --- | --- |
| Routine service letter to one customer | The staff member's Branch Manager |
| Overdue-payment reminder | The Branch Manager (collections-contact-rules) |
| Anything to more than 50 customers at once | Corporate Communications |
| Anything to a partner, vendor or the media | Corporate Communications and Compliance |
| Anything to a regulator | Compliance only |

The person who drafted a message may be its approver only where the table names their role.

## Drafts prepared with tools

A draft written with the help of a template engine or an AI assistant is still a draft: it is checked line by line
against the source data and approved under the table above before it is sent (see ai-assistant-use). It may
contain only data the sender is already allowed to see.

## Records

A copy of every approved outgoing communication is kept on the customer or case file (record-retention).
